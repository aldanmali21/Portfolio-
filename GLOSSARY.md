# Portfolio

A brief, single-page personal portfolio for a software developer, built to win freelance and agency work.

## Language

**Client**:
A freelancer's or agency's prospective customer: the person or organization who would hire the developer for project work. The portfolio's primary audience.
_Avoid_: Recruiter, employer, visitor, hiring manager

**Case study**:
A featured piece of past work, presented as the problem, what was built, and the outcome.
_Avoid_: Project card, work sample, gallery item

**Signature interaction**:
The one interactive piece on the page that demonstrates a real skill, in place of decorative animation.
_Avoid_: Animation, effect, gimmick
